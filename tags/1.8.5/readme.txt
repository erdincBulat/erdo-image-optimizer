Next-Gen WebP/AVIF Converter, Image SEO & Auditor. Professional Image Management for your WordPress Media Library.

== Description ==

Erdo Image Optimizer is not just another compression tool; it is a comprehensive Image SEO & Performance Suite. While most plugins only focus on file size, we bridge the gap between performance and search engine visibility.

Our unique "Image SEO Auditor" scans your entire media library and provides a professional health score (0/6) for every single image. We help you identify and fix the most common SEO mistakes that developers often overlook, such as messy filenames, missing alt texts, and incorrect titles.

Why Erdo Image Optimizer?

* SEO-First Auditing: A professional 6-point scoring system that tells you exactly why your image isn't ranking.
* Smart Filename Sanitization: Automatically transforms messy names like "IMG_1234.JPG" into SEO-friendly "professional-web-design.webp" during upload.
* Next-Gen Performance: Seamless WebP and AVIF conversion to boost your Google PageSpeed Insights scores.
* Library Cleanup: Identify and remove unused images that bloat your server and confuse search crawlers.

== Key Features ==
* One-Click WebP and AVIF Conversion: Drastically reduce file sizes for better performance without losing quality.
* SEO Health Auditor: Enforce SEO rules for filenames, Alt Text, and Titles with a professional scoring system.
* EXIF Data Stripper: Automatically remove sensitive camera metadata to reduce file weight and protect privacy.
* Automatic Filename Sanitization: Make your filenames SEO-friendly during upload by removing spaces and special characters.
* One-Click SEO Alignment: Quickly sync your image alt tags with your post titles for perfect relevance.
* Modern Dashboard: A clean and horizontal interface designed for professional productivity.

== Installation ==

1. Upload the erdo-image-optimizer folder to the /wp-content/plugins/ directory.
2. Activate the plugin through the 'Plugins' menu in WordPress.
3. Access 'Image Optimizer' from your sidebar to begin your first SEO audit.
