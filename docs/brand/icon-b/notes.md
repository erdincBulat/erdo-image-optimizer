# Icon B: the "e" that is a photo

- **Idea:** a lowercase geometric "e" drawn as one rounded-square stroke, open at the lower right. Its upper counter is a small framed picture: a lighter "sky" field sitting on an amber horizon line, which is also the e's crossbar. Letter and image are the same shape, so it works as the Erdo brand mark too.
- **Grid:** 256-unit viewBox, 16-unit grid. Stroke centerline runs 64 to 192 on both axes, corner radius 40 (inner 28), letter stroke 24, horizon 16 (thinner on purpose so it reads as a line, not a slab). Tile radius 56. All paths are hand-written; no font.
- **Palette:** tile `#0f5257` (deep pine teal: calm and trustworthy, clearly apart from WordPress blue `#2271b1` and from the purple/blue "AI" look), sky `#1a6a6e` (the same hue one step lighter, used only inside the counter), letter `#ffffff`, horizon `#f0b429` (warm amber, the single accent: daylight on a photo, also a quiet "pass" colour). The dark tile carries the mark on white and `#f0f0f1`; on `#1d2327` the white e and the amber line keep it readable.
- **Motion:** only the horizon moves. Over about 1.4 s of a 6 s loop it wipes out to the right and sweeps back in from the left, like a scan line passing over the picture, then holds. It is one dash-offset run from 0 to -200 (one full dash period), so time 0 and the rest state are the same complete icon. Ease is `cubic-bezier(.65,0,.35,1)`. `prefers-reduced-motion` turns it off.
- **At 32 px:** the stroke is 3 px and the horizon 2 px. The open "e" silhouette carries recognition, the amber line still shows, and the sky tint merges into the tile without muddying it.
