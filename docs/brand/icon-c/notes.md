# Icon C, revision 2: the image gets its words (and its score)

- **Idea:** a bright photo print (white border, sky, sun, two hills) with its caption written underneath (alt text / filename bars). A round SEO score badge is stuck on the print's corner and fills up as the words are written.
- **Grid:** 256 viewBox, 16-unit grid, tile rx 52. Print 192x120 at (32,32) with an 8-unit white border; photo 176x104 inside it (rx 12). Caption bars 16 high at y 168 and 200 (112 and 80 wide). Badge centred on the print's corner (192,152): white disc r40, cut out from the print by an 8-unit ring in the tile colour, score ring r24 / stroke 12, up chevron stroke 6.
- **`icon.svg` (cobalt):** tile and far hill `#2856E8` (bright cobalt, clearly bluer and more saturated than WP `#2271b1`), sky `#9ED4FF`, sun / low score / caret `#FFB020` (amber), near hill / good score `#1FC46B` (green). Plus white and a neutral ring track `#E3E7EC`.
- **`icon-alt.svg` (teal + coral):** tile, far hill and good score `#0FA98F` (teal), sky / caret / mid score `#FFD45E` (sunny yellow), sun / low score `#FF6B4A` (coral), near hill `#08765F` (deep green). Plus white and `#E3E7EC`.
- **Why it pops:** both tiles are saturated, mid-light colours with a white print and white caption on top, so they hold up on white, #f0f0f1 and #1d2327 alike. No gradients, glows or shadows.
- **Motion (6 s loop, all motion in the first ~2.5 s, then hold):** line 1 drops back to a short, faded bar (the camera name) and the score ring drops to about 20% in amber/coral. A caret writes line 1 out, then line 2, and the ring fills alongside it: amber, then yellow, then green at about 92%. A small up chevron rises into the badge. The caret pulses once and disappears. Frame 0 is the same as the rest state, so the PNGs and `prefers-reduced-motion` both show the finished icon.
- **At 32 px:** the white-bordered photo (sky, sun and hills) reads clearly as a picture. You also get two white caption lines and a white badge with a green ring on its rim. The chevron inside the badge is lost below about 64 px.
- **Revisions from the screenshots:** the far hill used the tile colour, so the photo bled into the background. The white print border fixed that and also says "photo" more strongly. The badge was too small at 32 px, so it went from r36 / stroke 9 to r40 / stroke 12. In the alt palette, the sun was white on yellow (too faint), so it became coral, and the ring's colour change now passes through yellow instead of muddy grey.
- About 2.7 KB each, CSS keyframes only, no JS, fonts or external refs.
