# Icon A — "the frame that tightens"

- **Idea:** four white crop corners close in on a flat image tile while the tile's darker corner slice shrinks. The crop says *precise and correct*, the shrinking slice says *lighter file*. It shows lean and tidy images with no "magic" metaphor and no mountain/sun photo glyph.
- **Grid:** 256 viewBox on a 16-unit grid. The corner strokes are 16 wide (centrelines at 40/216, arms 56 long), the gap to the tile is 16, the tile is 128×128 at 64–192, and the rest-state slice is a 64-unit right triangle. Everything is full-bleed and flat: no radius, shadow or gradient.
- **Palette:** deep teal `#0f5e57` as the field, amber `#f3b23a` for the image tile, burnt amber `#d9822b` for the slice, and white `#ffffff` for the crop marks. Teal reads as calm and trustworthy, and it is not WordPress blue or the usual SaaS purple. Amber is the warm "photo" colour, and the two-tone amber keeps the fold legible without a third hue. The teal field holds its own on both white and `#1d2327` (checked in preview.html).
- **Motion (6 s loop, CSS only):** at 0 s the corners sit 24 units further out, the tile is at 125% and the slice at 150%. Over about 1.5 s the corners ease in with a 2-unit overshoot and settle, while the tile and slice shrink to size. The icon then holds still until 86% (about 3.6 s of rest), and over the last 0.8 s it eases back out so the loop has no snap. There is no rotation and no opacity flashing, so it can't read as a spinner. `prefers-reduced-motion` shows the static rest state.
- **Static states:** the markup with no animation is the rest state, so PNG fallbacks can be rendered straight from the file. The t=0 frame (wide frame, big tile) also works as a complete icon.
- **At 32 px:** the corners render as about 2 px white L-shapes and the tile as a 16 px amber square, so the shape reads as "cropped image" at a glance. The slice becomes a small darker corner: a subtle extra, not essential.
- **Weak spot:** corner brackets on their own echo the generic "scan / focus" symbol. The amber tile with its fold is what makes the icon ownable, so don't drop it.
